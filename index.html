Abstract

This corrected technical note reports the reproducibility audit of the FT01-A pipeline, scripts v04-v07, on the public UCI Gas Sensor Array Drift at Different Concentrations dataset. Version 1.1 preserves the original clean-rerun identity and adds a corrective recomputation for comparator defects identified retrospectively in v05 and v06. In v05, source-transfer and target-oracle errors are now compared on the same target holdout support, and the pairwise same-batch diagonal is evaluated on the local holdout rather than on rows that include training data. The corrected v05 diagonal is 1.000 by construction, while the off-diagonal median remains 11.977. The corrected v05 all-test same-support transfer/oracle raw-row median is 18.502. In v06, corrected same-support transfer/oracle medians remain materially above 1 for Ridge and Random Forest under raw and mean/std alignment. Version 1.1 also clarifies that v04 value 5.018 is the median of cell-level future/local MAE ratios, removes methods not actually implemented in v05, and removes the v1.0 reference to TCA-based adaptation because the v07 package does not implement TCA. For the exact feature matrices, batch splits, models, seeds, and implemented controls tested, later-batch errors remain materially higher than same-batch/local or valid same-support target-oracle baselines. No general claim is made about calibration theory, sensor reliability, or drift compensation.

Correction and scope. This is an access copy of the previously published Zenodo corrected version v1.1, not a new scientific publication or peer-reviewed journal article. The v1.0 record remains preserved as a historical version. The conclusions are specific to the exact FT01-A dataset and pipeline; the correction restricts comparator interpretation.
